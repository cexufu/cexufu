# Cexu (Tony) Fu

**AI governance, grounded in mechanisms.** I use source-level and code-level evidence (information flow, attribution and interventions inside LLMs) to answer real AI governance problems.

PhD researcher, EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Cyber Security, University of Bristol.

[Website](https://cexufu.github.io) · [Blog: Mechanistic Governance](https://cexufu.github.io/blog/) · [Email](mailto:fucx9501@outlook.com) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cexu_fu)

## What I work on

- **Tracing where outputs come from.** Attributing model outputs to the inputs and internal computations that drive them, and checking those attributions with ablations and interventions.
- **Detecting sensitive-information leakage.** Following confidential content as it moves through a model or an agent.
- **Turning governance questions into testable ones.** Which claims about model behaviour can be verified, and how.

## Background

- **Tencent**: measuring information cocoons, polarisation and AIGC prevalence; semi-automated data-protection impact assessments
- **Kuaishou**: content policy, risk and data governance, privacy
